# Privacy Policy - Focus for YouTube

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Focus for YouTube does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- The extension makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking
  or advertising code.
- The only data it saves are your four on/off preferences (hide Shorts, open
  Subscriptions instead of Home, hide recommended videos, hide comments). They
  are stored with the browser's built-in extension storage and, if you have
  browser sync enabled, synced between your own browsers by the browser
  vendor. The developer has no access to them.
- The extension runs only on www.youtube.com and m.youtube.com, where it hides
  page elements and redirects two kinds of page loads. It does not read or
  record what you watch.

Focus for YouTube is an independent project and is not affiliated with,
endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC.

Source code and contact: https://github.com/Shamiaza/focus-for-youtube (open an issue for any question).
